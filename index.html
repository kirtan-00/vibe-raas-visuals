Visuals for the LED screens

01

Bandhani Tunnel

Fly into rings of bandhani. Every dhol hit fires a gold ring and a camera punch.
02

Bandhani Stripes

Bandhani bands scroll and swell on each drum hit. Press 2 for the mandala.
03

Ghoomar Mandala

A bandhani mandala that spins with the tempo and flares on every hit.

For the VJ

  1. Open this page in Chrome on the laptop going to the LED.
  2. Pick a visual. Drag the window onto the LED display.
  3. Click once and press Allow for the microphone. Use the mixer line out if you can, or the laptop mic.
  4. Press F for fullscreen. It reacts to drum beats only.
